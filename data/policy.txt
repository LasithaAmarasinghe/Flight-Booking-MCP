AIRLINE TRAVEL & BOOKING POLICIES:
1. Baggage:
   - Economy: 1 carry-on (up to 7kg) + 1 checked bag (up to 23kg).
   - Excess baggage is charged at $40/kg at the gate.
2. Changes & Cancellations:
   - Cancellations made > 48 hours prior to departure receive a 90% refund.
   - Cancellations within 48 hours incur a $150 cancellation penalty.
3. Seat Selection:
   - Standard seats are free during check-in (open 24h prior).
   - Extra legroom seats are $45 USD flat fee.
4. Booking:
   - A booking is confirmed only after payment details have been successfully
     processed and a booking reference has been issued.
   - Passengers must provide their full legal name as shown on their travel
     document.
   - Each passenger may book up to 9 seats in a single reservation.
5. Check-in & Travel Documents:
   - Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 90 minutes
     before departure.
   - Passengers should arrive at the airport at least 3 hours before
     international departures.
   - A valid passport, visa, and any required health or entry documents are the
     passenger's responsibility.
6. Flight Changes:
   - Date or route changes are subject to availability and any fare
     difference.
   - Changes must be requested before the scheduled departure time.
   - No-show passengers are not eligible for a refund or flight change.
7. Special Assistance:
   - Requests for wheelchair assistance, dietary accommodations, or other
     special services should be made at least 48 hours before departure.
   - Infants and unaccompanied minors may be subject to additional restrictions
     and fees.
